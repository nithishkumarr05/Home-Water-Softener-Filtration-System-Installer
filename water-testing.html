Why It Matters

Why water testing matters

You can't fix what you can't see. Water problems — hardness, chlorine, sediment, and contaminants — are invisible until they start causing damage or health issues. Testing reveals the full picture.

Protect your family's health

Identify contaminants like lead, bacteria, and heavy metals before they cause harm.

Protect your plumbing & appliances

Hard water scale silently damages pipes, water heaters, dishwashers, and washing machines.

Save money with the right system

Test results tell us exactly what you need — so you never overpay for the wrong equipment.

Enjoy better taste & clarity

Discover the source of odd tastes, smells, or cloudiness — and how to fix it.

What We Test

Parameters we analyze

Our comprehensive test covers 8+ key water quality indicators.

Water Hardness

Measures calcium & magnesium levels that cause scale, dry skin, and dull laundry.

Chlorine & Chloramine

Detects disinfection chemicals that affect taste, odor, and skin comfort.

pH Level

Identifies acidic or alkaline water that can corrode plumbing or taste off.

Total Dissolved Solids

Measures overall mineral & particle content for water purity baseline.

Heavy Metals

Screens for lead, copper, iron, and other metals that pose health risks.

Bacteria & Coliforms

Detects harmful microorganisms — especially important for well water homes.

Sediment & Turbidity

Measures cloudiness, sand, rust, and particles suspended in your water.

Odor & Taste Compounds

Identifies sulfur, musty, or chemical smells that make water unpleasant.
